## Decided

- Names match the invite: Pregame (not Week or Puzzle Week), Games (not the Game day tab or Gauntlet), Extra points (not Quests or Side quests), time boost (not the Continue), stars only (no Bronze, Silver or Gold).
- On Games: "Stage score" for the player stat, "Run progress" for the stars so far, "Stage timeline" for the log of who did what.
- Menu titled Game Day: Standings, Pregame, Games, Extra points, then the invite and Host (only arckc). Your name and Sign out sit at the bottom. The More page goes away.
- Standings shows Overall only, with stacked bars per source (Puzzles violet, Trackmania orange, Marathon teal). No event tabs; each event's table lives on its own page.
- Pregame tabs: Puzzles, Trackmania, Marathon. RL 1v1 is removed.
- Puzzles: one day at a time with previous, next and Back to today; one card per puzzle; a green Post results button opens a sheet (phone) or dialog (desktop); edit in place from your own row; drafts kept on errors; inline delete confirmation (the RBK-47 fix).
- Marathon counts your personal best in any mode.
- A game in progress: tap the goal you reached to set stars; End is the button, and the time boost, Plan B and swap sit under More.
